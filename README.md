# Mechanic Trust

AI product case study for a consumer mobile app that helps drivers understand car issues, judge whether a repair quote is fair, and find trustworthy mechanics.

## What problem this solves

Auto repair is a high-anxiety, high-information-asymmetry category. When a driver gets a repair quote, they usually have no reliable way to know:
- whether the repair is necessary,
- whether the quoted price is fair,
- or which nearby mechanic is actually trustworthy.

Mechanic Trust is designed to close that trust gap through three layers:
1. AI-powered issue assessment from photo, voice, or text input
2. Transparent local price ranges tied to repair type
3. Trust-ranked shops with social proof signals

## Product thesis

Mechanic Trust combines AI-powered issue assessment, transparent price guidance, and trust-ranked mechanic recommendations to reduce anxiety and improve decision confidence.
